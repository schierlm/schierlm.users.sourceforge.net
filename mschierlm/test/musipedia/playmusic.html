Play with keyboard

Keyboard layout:

Press the keys on your keyboard's main row (a,s,d,f on US keyboards) for playing notes. Shift keys increases tone by one octave. Use the space bar to make the last note longer (i. e. press the space bar for every quarter note this note is longer than a quarter note, or use it as a "metronome"), or digits 1-9 to change last note length. Use 0 to insert a rest. Cursor keys move cursor or change pitch of last note.
